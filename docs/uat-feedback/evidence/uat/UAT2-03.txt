KICH BAN UAT2-03: Kiem tra cat tia khoang trang CV URL va phong ve URL (FB-03)
Nguoi thuc hien: SV-02 (Sinh vien thao tac nop CV)
Thoi diem: 2026-11-03 10:40:52

Buoc 1: Nop don voi CV URL co khoang trang o dau va cuoi ('   https://drive.google.com/file/d/test-cv/view   '):
HTTP Status nhan duoc: 200
Don ung tuyen duoc tao thanh cong voi Ma: 1
Gia tri CvUrl thuc te luu trong CSDL: https://drive.google.com/file/d/test-cv/view

Buoc 2: Nop don voi URL scheme ftp://:
HTTP Status nhan duoc: 400 (Ky vong: 400)

Buoc 3: Nop don voi URL co khoang trang o giua ('https://example.com/cv file.pdf'):
HTTP Status nhan duoc: 400 (Ky vong: 400)

Ket luan: DAT (PASS) - He thong tu dong cat tia khoang trang 2 dau hop le va luu chuoi sach vao CSDL; tu choi triet de scheme ftp va khoang trang giua.
